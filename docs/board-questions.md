# Board Questions For AI Oversight

## Value And Evidence

- Which user, workforce, or service outcome is expected to move, and against what baseline?
- What evidence would cause us to stop or narrow the system?
- Are reported productivity gains net of review, rework, incidents, and displaced effort?

## Accountability

- Who owns the decision made with or by the system?
- Which decisions may the system execute, recommend, or never influence?
- Can affected people reach a responsible human with authority to act?

## Capability And Control

- Do evaluations represent difficult and consequential operating conditions?
- Which controls have been tested independently rather than asserted by the supplier?
- How quickly can the organization detect, contain, and learn from a failure?

## Strategic Resilience

- Which data, model, platform, and supplier dependencies are hard to replace?
- How do incentives change when multiple autonomous systems interact?
- What capability must remain inside the organization even when technology is outsourced?

## Portfolio Governance

- Which material AI systems are missing from this view?
- Are review depth and executive attention proportional to consequence and uncertainty?
- What did incidents and near misses change in standards, evaluations, or investment?
